## Exact Splash peer-receipt gate

Splash is an optional offer broadcaster in this candidate. A successful POST
to CATalyst's local Splash node proves local submission only. The upstream
[Splash usage documentation](https://github.com/dexie-space/splash) describes
the separate `--offer-hook` on a receiving peer; connected-peer metrics alone
also do not prove delivery of a particular offer.

For the live acceptance run, use two independent Splash nodes with distinct
identities and storage, one attached to the authorized CATalyst TEST 7 maker
and one on the receiving PC. Before any offer creation, complete the current
campaign/fee approval and action-time Sage identity, balance, pending-offer,
safety, campaign, and ledger checks. Record the exact active offer trade ID,
asset ID, side, and SHA-256 of its `offer1` bytes at the maker. Record the
local Splash submission response separately. At the receiving peer's hook,
record the delivery time and SHA-256 of the received `offer1` bytes and verify
they match the maker's active offer; confirm the receiving node is a distinct
peer and the CAT/XCH pair is the expected MZ asset. A local response, a peer
count, a synthetic hook payload, or an offer seen only on Dexie earns no
Splash peer-receipt credit. After the observation, use the normal authorized
cancel/reconcile path and verify Sage and CATalyst reach terminal agreement.
This gate remains open until those exact-offer records exist.

The receiving PC can use `tests/manual/splash_peer_receiver.py` as a
loopback-only hook witness. Its `--self-test` sends a synthetic local POST; a
live run requires `--port` and `--output` and records only the received offer's
UTC time, UTF-8 byte length, and SHA-256 in fsynced JSONL. Configure the
receiving Splash node's `--offer-hook` to
`http://127.0.0.1:<port>/offer`, and capture that node's distinct peer identity
separately. The probe is prepared and self-tested; it has not received a real
peer offer and earns no peer-receipt credit yet.
