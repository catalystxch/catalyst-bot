# Startup wizard scroll and focus: exact `96d5908` candidate

Draft PR [#220](https://github.com/catalystxch/catalyst-bot/pull/220) remains targeted at `main`. This note records two frontend defects found during isolated public-readiness UI inspection. The exact runtime/source commit is `96d5908d8b7c4b57ba4dadf68b2375a4d8fe0ea2`; parent `94159265ae434e8eba2a712eddb90e5aa5d80c3d` introduced the scroll reset.

## Reproduction and root cause

At a 600×400 viewport, the Risk Disclosure needs vertical scrolling to expose its buttons. After continuing, the shorter Connect Wallet step retained the prior overlay scroll position. In a real isolated browser session, the new step opened with the top branding partly off-screen; `#startupOverlay.scrollTop` was 46 despite the new step beginning. The browser connected only to an isolated Flask shell on port 5099 with `CMM_DATA_DIR=E:\catalyst-f0e1e97-ui-inspection\data`; its Connect to Sage button was **not** used.

`startupAcceptRiskDisclosure()` hides the disclosure and advances with `startupSetPhase('wallet_choice', ...)`. `startupSetPhase()` changed text and visibility but left the persistent scroll container at its previous position. A focused Chromium regression first failed with `scrollTop == 76` where zero was required. The fix resets the overlay scroll only when the startup phase changes, preserving the user's position during repeated status updates within the same phase.

After that correction, the same real isolated browser showed `document.activeElement` as `BODY` after Continue. Keyboard focus had escaped the visible modal. A focused real-Flask Chromium regression failed with `#startupOverlay.contains(document.activeElement) == false`. The `96d5908` child focuses the overlay after either Wallet Choice result is shown, without scrolling it. The focused test then passed, and the real 600×400 browser showed `activeId=startupOverlay`, focus inside the dialog, and `scrollTop=0`. No Sage connection or wallet action was made.
