This episode shows that a snapshot can coincide with a monitor timeout and sampler delay **without an observed terminal lease failure**. It does not prove the lease stayed continuously valid during the unsampled gap, nor does it identify the blocked instruction in the earlier Veeam-overlap failure. Snapshot initiation alone is therefore insufficient to explain that failure.
