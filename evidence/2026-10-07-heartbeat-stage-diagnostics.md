# Mutation heartbeat stage diagnostics after the Veeam-overlap safety stop

The original TEST 7 `f0e1e97` app failed closed with `HEARTBEAT_FAILED` during a Veeam snapshot overlap. Its heartbeat took 24,985 ms inside one durable database attempt, but the available log did not distinguish opening the SQLite connection, acquiring the write transaction, reading the lease, finishing the transaction, or closing the connection. See [the failure chronology](2026-10-07-veeam-snapshot-heartbeat-f0e1e97.md). The exact blocked instruction and whether VSS alone caused the pause remain unknown.

This isolated diagnostic change adds bounded integer millisecond measurements for `connection`, `begin`, `read`, `finish`, and `close` stages to the existing mutation-heartbeat timing log. It preserves the heartbeat result returned to callers, the 30-second lease, all expiry and compare-and-set checks, and the terminal fail-closed stop. No wallet identifiers, exception messages, paths or SQL parameters enter the new fields. The stage measurements should make a recurrence actionable without weakening mutation authorization.

Two focused regressions were red before the implementation and green after it: a deliberately slow connection on a successful heartbeat, and a connection delayed past lease expiry that still fails closed and records its stage. The affected mutation-gate, stability-schema and long-gap-recovery suites passed **472 tests** in 183.06 seconds on Windows. Ruff check and format passed on the three changed files. This diagnostic change does **not** establish a safe availability fix for host stalls or satisfy either 24-hour release gate.
